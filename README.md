# Token desk

This is our token platform management desk. We launch tokens from nothing, keep the work in one overview, and stay on the token after it is live.

## Launch

A token launches in one of two ways:

- On a launchpad. Launchpads here are always a bonding curve.
- Straight on the blockchain. That choice is On-chain, with no launchpad.

## Before launch

The token needs an overview of every detail and its metadata before it goes out.

On that same token we assign the phases and the packages and services that apply, and we keep a budget overview. The phases are Startup, Pre-launch, Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3.

Packages and services come from contacts. A contact is tied to the desk they sell, and that desk is where their packages and separate services live. We put the contact on the token, then add the pieces that launch is using. The contact card shows what is already on the plan and what is still open.

## Budget

Buying supply costs money. The desk keeps a card for 30%, 40%, 50%, and 60%, on each route, so the token budget shows the ETH cost before launch.

Adding volume also loses gas. Volume cards run from 100K to 1M. The price on the card is the budget, which is not lost. Gas is listed beside it as the loss. Each card uses a $1,000 average swap and $5 of gas per swap. The 500K card is a $5,000 budget, with $2,500 gas lost on 500 swaps.

## After launch

Once the token is live we keep an eye on volume, market cap, and the rest of the market picture.
